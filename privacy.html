Privacy policy: Brain YouTube reader

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Brain YouTube reader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Atakan Taşkın, on his own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the list of playlists and the videos in them on the owner's own YouTube channel, and it can create one playlist named "Brain" on that channel. It does not post, comment, upload, delete or change anything else.

What it stores

Video titles, links and public transcripts are saved in the owner's private notes on his own computer and his private repositories. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's password keychain.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. No other users' data is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
